Questions

Good to know.

Do candidates need an account?

No. They open your link, fill in the form, upload a PDF resume and submit.

How do I get access?

Sign up with your agency name, mobile number, email and password. Our team reviews your request and, after your subscription is confirmed, sends you a unique access code. You log in with your email, password and that code.

Is HirePool free?

No. HirePool is a paid subscription. See the plans above, or contact sales for 6-month plans.

Who can see the resumes?

Only your agency. Files are private and opened through short-lived secure links.

What file types are supported?

PDF resumes, with a size limit shown on the application form.

How long are job posts and resumes kept?

Each job post and all its candidates are deleted automatically 10 days after posting. Shortlist the people you want and download them from your dashboard before then.
